# Privacy Policy for Cassiel (Finance Tracker)

**Last updated:** August 10, 2026

Cassiel ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Cassiel (Finance Tracker) mobile application.

## 1. Our Privacy Approach
Cassiel is an ad-free personal finance tracker designed with user privacy in mind. To monitor active installations and support admin analytics, the app collects minimal, non-sensitive device telemetry. The app does not display advertisements, does not contain third-party ad networks, and keeps all details of your financial logs strictly confidential on your device.

## 2. Information We Collect and Process
To help us understand app usage and maintain the application, we process the following data types:

* **Financial Data (Stored Locally Only)** — Detailed records of your transactions, income, expenses, custom categories, budgets, and account balances are stored exclusively on your device (local database / local storage). This sensitive financial data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or any third party.
* **Telemetry Data (Transmitted to Firebase)** — To monitor active installations, we collect basic device telemetry which is stored securely on our Firebase server. This includes:
  - An anonymous unique device ID
  - Your profile name (display name)
  - Device details (OS, brand/model, and browser type)
  - Installation and last active timestamps
  - Total count of transactions recorded (only the quantity of records, NOT the content/values of the transactions)
  - Current app version
* **No Authentication Required** — Cassiel does not require you to register an account, sign in, or provide personal identifying info (like email or phone number) to be used.

## 3. Network Access & Third-Party Services
The app requests the Internet permission to securely transmit the basic telemetry data described above to our Firebase database and to support local notification checks/app updates. We do not sell or share this telemetry data with third parties.

## 4. Data Security
Because all data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device's security. We recommend protecting your device with a PIN, password, fingerprint, or face recognition, and keeping your OS up to date.

## 5. Data Deletion
Since Cassiel does not store any data on external servers, you can permanently delete all app data at any time by:
* Clearing the app's data/cache in your device settings, or
* Uninstalling the app.

There is no server-side data to request deletion of, because none exists.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Cassiel does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because the app does not collect data at all. The app is safe for general audiences.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
